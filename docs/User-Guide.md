# Steadier user guide

Steadier holds everything you're responsible for, then shows you a manageable few at a time. It helps you pick the next thing, stay with it, and come back when you drift. Everything is in Adelaide time.

## The big ideas

- **Tasks** are anything you need to do. Each task can have a next step, dates, a project, and notes. (Tasks used to have an **Area**, such as Home or Business. Area was removed because Pillars do that job. Any Area you'd chosen was copied into the task's notes as a line like `Area: Home`. There's also no Important flag: Steadier doesn't rank tasks.)
- **Booked anchors** are tasks with a set day and clock time, such as a shift, an appointment or a job. Only these appear as blocks on the Calendar, and only these get spoken check-ins.
- **Next thing** is the one task you've chosen to do now. Finish it with **Done**, or put it back with **Not now**.
- **Roles**: every task is either **Imperative** (chosen work that moves your life forward) or **Support** (keeps life running). A task or project linked to an active Imperative is in the Imperative row automatically, shown as **Imperative (linked)**. Anything not linked keeps the role you chose. If that Imperative is retired, it goes back to your own choice.
- **Patterns**: every task is either **Timely** (has a moment that matters) or **Sustained** (needs regular attention over time).
- **Imperatives** are the few focus areas that matter most right now.
- **Compass** is your bigger picture: **Pillars** (the big parts of your life), **Goals** under them, and **Goal Outcomes** that projects work towards.
- **The task card** has three dots: **Action** (you've confirmed the title reads as something to do), **Place** (a project, Life Admin or Erosion Guard) and **First step** (one small physical action, or tap **Title is the step**). **When** (a date or deadline) is optional and has no dot. While any dot is unlit the card is **faded** (not ready). When all three are lit it turns **solid** and the task is ready. A missing dot never stops you saving.
- **Front of Mind** holds what's on your mind that isn't a task yet: ideas, concerns, things you're weighing. No dates, timers or warnings.
- **Suggestions** Steadier makes (like a role or a project link) stay marked as suggested until you confirm them.

## Getting around

Open the menu to move between areas. The main ones are **My life today**, **Calendar**, **All work** and **Weekly Reality Review**. On other pages, tap the **i** button at the top for a short "What this page is for" note and the page's own explanation.

On a phone, five tabs sit at the bottom: **Today · Overview · All work · Calendar · Projects**. Backups and Compass are in the menu, and Compass is also linked from Overview.

The **+** button at the right of the tab bar is the one capture button: **tap** to type a task, **hold** to talk to the voice assistant. It never covers a card.

**Paste anything:** paste an email, message or messy list into the **+** box. When it looks like several things, tap **Split into items**. Steadier removes account numbers, logins and passwords (addresses are kept), sends only the cleaned text to Claude, and shows each item dashed with a tick box: Task, Idea, Bill, Cancel by, Note or List, with its date. Tap one to change its words, type or date. A quiet grey line says when one looks like a task you already have. Nothing saves until you tap **Save N ticked**; long pastes are never auto-saved as one task.

## Areas of the app

### My life today
The **Today** tab is for doing. It uses three words only: **Now · Next · Later**, in time order. Nothing is ranked.
- **Check-in (weekdays):** when there's a question for you, one line sits above Now, showing the question. Tap it to answer: the morning check-in (from 08:00) asks where tasks overdue for placement should live, and on Mondays about weekend changes; the evening one (from 17:00) asks whether today's dated tasks are done. Answer each one, or tap **Later**; either way the line folds away until the next check-in. No line at weekends, before 08:00, or when there's nothing to ask. It's a question, never a count.
- **Now:** the booked block running now, with its likely finish, a red now mark, and big **Done** / **Finished early** buttons. If nothing is running, it's your running focus, your chosen Next thing, or the next booking as **Up next in X min**, each with **Start** and **Done**. With nothing booked, pick a Next thing right there in the card (tap a task, check its first small action, **Make it my Next thing**). You're never sent to another screen.
- **Next:** your chosen Next thing, or else the next booking.
- **Later:** everything else dated or booked for today. Undated work is never on Today.
- **Slipped:** a block past its likely finish shows **Keep going** (stretches it to its likely finish), **Move** or **Drop** (off today, kept in All work). If it slips again and you leave it, it's listed in the Weekly Review (Capacity step).
- **Blocked by:** a booked task waiting on an unfinished prerequisite offers **Move it** or **Remove prerequisite**.
- **Needs attention (n)** opens Overview: past-due work, review due, Sort, Link check, unplaced, Claude requests. This is the only count in Steadier: one row per kind, and the total is the sum of the rows (a task can count in more than one row).
- Tap any row to open its sheet: notes, outcome, prerequisites, subtasks. The **i** button explains the signals.

**Signals (everywhere):** a green left bar means it serves an Imperative. Dashed means suggested (tap to confirm). Faded with dots means not set up. Grey chips show only what's off normal: ⏱ +20m / −10m, slipped, overdue 2d, moved 3×, quiet 9d, review due. A chip turns red only when it will clash with a fixed booking or deadline. The broken-link icon means a project, or a task in it, is missing its Goal Outcome or Imperative link (also in Link check). The old shape-and-colour arrows are gone.

### Overview
Overview is for seeing, top to bottom:
- **Needs attention (n):** one row per kind (Review due, Link check, Weekly review, Sort, Unplaced, Upkeep due, Past due, Claude requests). Upkeep due opens in place to list each item. Only rows above zero show, and the total matches Today's.
- **Next thing:** your chosen one, and the Pool to choose from (Not now, Still on and Drop work as before).
- **Today:** booked count and free time, with a thin strip of your day and the red now mark. Tap it for Calendar.
- **Moving:** up to three active projects with their signals, then **All projects**.
- **Held and waiting**, then **Why:** Compass, Pillars, Front of Mind (no number) and Movement.

### Calendar
Day, 3-day, week, month and **Board** views of your booked anchors (tasks with a day and a clock time). Blocks show the title first; the green left bar marks Imperative work. Week shows titles only, with "Nh free" under each date. Explanations are in the ⋮ menu under **How this calendar works**.
- **Board** lists the day's bookings like a departure board: time · task · status. Status shows only when something is off: Done, Due, Now, Slipped, or the likely finish (e.g. "~10:55 · +10m") when it's 10 or more minutes late. It's red only if that runs into the next booking or a deadline.
- This is where you **change a booked time**: open the task and change its day, time or length.
- Untimed tasks and flexible windows are not shown as blocks.
- Month view also shows bills and renewals due, birthdays, and anchors you've marked as major.

### All work
Every task in one list, for finding things. Search, one filter, and groups by project (tap a group to fold it). Each project's **Outcome** shows once at the top of its group; tap it to read it all. Work with no project is under **No project**: Life Admin · Erosion Guard · Not placed.
- Rows are the same as Today: a round **Done** circle, the title, a grey date/time chip, at most two chips for anything off normal (blocked, overdue, moved…), the green bar for Imperative work, dashed for suggestions. Normal things show nothing.
- Tap a row to open its sheet: next action, prerequisites, notes, location and reminders. Choose the **Not ready** filter to see only faded cards.

### Sort
One minute a day. When you have faded tasks, **Needs attention** on Overview shows **Sort** with a count. Sort shows one faded card at a time: tap a big **Place** button (recent projects first, then Life Admin and Erosion Guard), say or type the **First step**, tap **It's an action**, then **Done** or **Skip**. After the last card you go back to Today.

### Front of Mind
What's on your mind that isn't a task yet: ideas, concerns, things you're weighing. (It replaced the Ideas shelf on 14 Nov 2026; every idea came across with its wording, date and history.) Open it from the menu or Overview (**Front of Mind ›**).
- **Add** with the **+** button and choose **Idea**, or say "Idea: …" to the voice assistant, or use Paste anything.
- **Tap an item** for the four actions, the same everywhere:
  - **Make it a task:** the usual unsaved task draft with the wording filled in. Once you save it, the item leaves the list, and its history says it became a task.
  - **Promote to Imperative:** saves a draft on the item only. Your next Weekly Review's Imperatives step offers **Add as Imperative** or **Not now**. Nothing changes your Imperatives until you say yes there.
  - **Keep:** it stays, marked as looked at today.
  - **Let go:** it moves under **Let go** with its history, and **Bring back** returns it.
- **Chips** show only what's off normal, two at most: **added by Claude** (until you Keep it), **suggested: Let go** (dashed, with Let go and Keep), and **quiet Nd** after 30 days without being looked at.
- **History** on each item shows every change, including earlier wording.
- Tasks dropped to Someday in the Weekly Review, and held obligation tasks, stay tasks: All work's **Someday** filter shows them.

### Review due / unplaced
Quick tidy-up: tasks due for a review, and tasks that don't have a place yet. It's for quick maintenance. The full weekly process is Weekly Reality Review.
The top card opens the Weekly Reality Review and says how long to allow: about 30–40 minutes for your first review, 15–25 minutes after that. Each unplaced task shows its age (for example **Unplaced · 19h**) and four ways to place it: **Project…**, **Imperative…**, **Life Admin**, **Erosion Guard**. Tasks due for a review have a **Reviewed** button (this acknowledges the review; it doesn't complete the work). Explanations sit behind the **i**; Read aloud is in the ⋮ menu.

### Waiting & blocked
Tasks waiting on another person or on something else first. Decide when to check back.

### Completed steps
History of what you've finished. Good for looking back. It isn't your to-do list.

### Project Outcomes
One compact row per project, grouped by Pillar (with **Not linked** and a folded **Achieved** group): title, "N open", grey chips only when something is off, and the green bar when it's linked to an Imperative. **List** or **Map** (by Goal Outcome).
Tap a project to open its page: **Next step** first, then **Why**, **Outcome** (tap to read it all) and **Tasks** with round Done circles (**Done** folded). Milestones, Observed evidence and Purpose are folded below. Empty fields show "+ Add …". **Mark outcome achieved** is at the bottom; edit the project from ⋮. A project is achieved only when you decide it is.

### My life matrix
**Balance, not ranking.** Four cells, each with a plain description: a chosen thing with a window; a chosen thing that grows through regular engagement; upkeep that falls due; upkeep that recurs. Each cell shows how many open tasks it holds, their estimated time, and time logged this week. The matrix only describes your work. It never changes the order of your suggestions.

### Imperatives
Your few current focus areas. Review them now and then, and retire ones that no longer deserve attention.

### Link check
Three short lists, shown as **Link check (n)** in Needs attention:

- **Imperatives with no work**: an active Imperative with no open task and no active project linked. Choose **Link work** (pick a project or task), **Not yet**, or **Step down** (Sustain, Delegate or Retire).
- **Imperative projects with no Imperative**: an active project in the Imperative row that isn't linked to an active Imperative. Choose **Link to an Imperative**, **Make it Support**, or **Not yet**.
- **Projects with no Goal Outcome**: an active project that no Goal Outcome has confirmed. Tap **Open project** and link it there.

Only links you made count. Suggested links show a **Confirm** button. **Not yet** holds an item until your next Weekly Reality Review, where you decide it again. Sustain or Delegate also holds it until then.

### Compass view, Compass map, and Pillars & Goals
- **Compass view**: active Imperatives and Projects at a glance, with status marks.
- **Compass map**: traces Pillars, Goals, Outcomes and Projects, and shows where a purpose link is missing.
- **Pillars & Goals**: where you add and edit the Pillar and Goal structure behind the map.

### Compass Library
Four reference links in the menu (Projects, Pillars, Goal Outcomes, Compass Library). Front of Mind's Notion page was retired when Front of Mind moved into Steadier. They open Notion in a new tab for now and will be swapped for server library links when Notion is retired. (The old Command Centre page is gone: its check-in is on Today, its Imperatives summary is Overview's Compass row, and its sync details are on Server & Compass.)

### Weekly Reality Review
The full weekly decision process: look at what happened, what's waiting, and what to change. The steps run Start → Unplaced → Front of Mind → Link check → Money → Action → Capacity → Imperatives → Map → Save. One line at the top shows **Step n of 10** with a thin bar; tap it to see the steps and jump to one. **Back** and **Next** move one step. Notes about how a step works sit behind the **i**, and Read aloud (in the ⋮ menu) reads the step you're on. Decisions you make are saved to the **Decision Log**. The **Link check** step shows everything you held with Not yet. Decide each one, or tap **Not yet again** to hold it one more week. Anything you leave comes back to Link check when you save the review. The **Front of Mind** step lists every item, Claude's items and suggestions first, each with Make it a task, Promote, Keep or Let go; choices apply when you save, and an item you skip stays as it was. Items promoted (or with a draft) appear in the **Imperatives** step under **From Front of Mind**, with **Add as Imperative** or **Not now**.

### Decision Log
Read-only list of decisions saved during Weekly Reality Reviews.

### Evidence Feed
Add dated evidence to a task or project, for example "spoke to the bank". Evidence never marks something Done by itself.

### Time / plans
Compares the time you have this week with the work you've planned, so you can see whether the plan fits.

### Near me
Tasks linked to saved places. Arrival alerts need location permission and Steadier open.

### Obligations
Bills, licences, renewals, insurance and tax dates, with lead time to prepare. It isn't accounting.
- **Cancel by:** a type for trials and subscriptions you may not want. Its date is the last day to cancel, and it reminds you 3 days before by default. Once you've dealt with it, tap **Done**. If you decide to keep it, change its type to Renewal.
- **Scan a bill:** take or pick a photo of a bill. Your server reads the text, removes anything that looks like account details, then sends only that cleaned text to Claude to pick out the provider, what it's for, amount, due date, how often and any renewal or cancel-by date. The photo is deleted as soon as it's read and is never stored or sent anywhere. You check every field, then **Save draft**. If the bill shows a second date, you can tick to add it too. (A photo you picked from your gallery stays in your gallery.)
- **Never stored:** account, card, BSB and reference numbers, biller codes, logins, passwords and addresses are thrown away from scans and from anything Claude adds. You'll see how many were removed. Pay using the bill or your bank app.
- **From Claude:** Claude can suggest an obligation (`add_my_life_obligation`). It arrives as a draft marked **Suggested by Claude · confirm**, with no reminder until you save it yourself.
- **Hide amounts from AI** (on by default): Claude can't see or add amounts. Scanned amounts still come from the cleaned text, so check them.
- There's no fortnightly option; a fortnightly bill is set to Does not repeat.
- **For an asset:** registration, insurance and the like can be linked to an asset (Freelander 2, the shack). They stay here and show on the asset's page; nothing is copied.

### Lists
Reusable checklists: packing, leaving the shack, the weekly shop. (Shopping Lists became Lists on 14 Nov 2026; every list, item and tick came across.)
- **A list is a template, and each use is a run.** The template is never ticked. **Start a run** to use it: the run has its own date and ticks. Editing the template updates a run in progress without losing ticks already made.
- **Reset** unticks everything on the current run (same run, same date). **New run** puts the current run into **Runs** with its date and starts a fresh one. Finished runs keep what they looked like, so you can see when you last packed for Kingston.
- **Type:** **Read-Do** (read each step, then do it; the run shows the next step large with **Done**) or **Do-Confirm** (do it from memory, then go down the list and confirm). **Use when** is the moment to use the list, e.g. "before leaving for Kingston".
- **Sections** group items. A section over 9 items gets a quiet dashed suggestion to split it; nothing is blocked.
- **One tick per item.** Edit, move and delete are behind each item's **⋯**. Add something **for this run only** at the bottom of a run.
- **Finish** closes the run. **Anything missing?** then offers this run's one-off items for the template in one tap, or type a new one.
- **Attach a list to a task or project** from its sheet or page (**+ Attach or link a list**). An attached list offers **Start the list too?** on the Now card, and its progress shows on the card as a grey chip ("Kingston packing 4/9"). If the card already has two chips, the progress shows as a quiet line inside the card instead, so "review due" and "moved" always stay. **Link only** just points at the list. Notes can link a list (the list icon on a note).
- Voice ("add eggs to the shopping list") and Paste anything add to the list's run after you confirm. Claude can only **suggest** items: they show dashed with **Confirm** or **Remove** until you decide.
- Wording tips are behind the **i**: state the result ("Gas – off"), not the action ("Check gas"). Read aloud, Undo and the theme are in the ⋮ menu.

### Assets & Upkeep
Physical things you look after: vehicles, places, equipment and appliances. (Assets & Wear Logs became Assets & Upkeep on 14 Nov 2026; the Freelander 2 record came across as a Vehicle with everything kept.)
- **The list** is grouped by type. Each card shows the name, a condition chip (Good, Fair, Poor, Needs attention; none until it's checked), one due chip when something is due, what's next and the last service. Retired assets are folded at the bottom.
- **Tap a card** for its page: meter, service plans, revisit, what a place holds, linked Obligations, and a history of log entries and readings with this year's cost.
- **Meter:** odometer (km) or engine hours, with dated readings. Any log entry can carry a reading.
- **Service plan:** a name and every so many months and/or km (or hours), whichever comes first. It shows last done and next due. Attach a list (say, "Car service drop-off") and a task made for that service gets the list attached, so starting it offers a run.
- **Revisit:** something to check by a date (roof and gutters by 30 Nov). **Record check** logs an Inspection with the condition you found, and can set the next revisit.
- **Log entry:** Service, Repair, Inspection or Note, with date, reading, cost, who did it, notes and condition after.
- **Places hold assets** (the shack holds the hot water system). A place's history includes what it holds.
- **Edit** is on the page; **Delete** is behind **⋯**. **Retire** is the quiet button at the bottom and keeps the history. Retiring a place asks whether to retire what it holds too (default: keep them active).
- **Upkeep due:** a service due within 14 days or 500 km (10 h), a revisit due within 14 days, or a vehicle or equipment reading over 60 days old shows once in Needs attention and in the Weekly Review's Action step. Nothing else reminds you. **Create task** opens an editable draft linked to the asset, with the due date filled in (today, if it's overdue); nothing is saved until you confirm. When you tick that task, Steadier asks **Log the service now?** (or **Record the check now?**) with today's date and the latest reading filled in. **Yes** logs it and the next one counts from then; **Not now** leaves it in Upkeep due, where **Log it done** is still there.
- **Costs:** the ⋮ menu has **Export costs (CSV)**: total per asset per year.
- Fuel, photos and warranty alerts aren't tracked; the warranty end date just shows under Details.

### Notes / Daily Log
Notes to yourself, one or more per day. A note never becomes a task unless you choose **convert to task**. Notes sync with the rest of Steadier and are included in backups.

### Site Cards
Reference cards and links for useful places and websites.

### Backups & trial
- **Download full backup** saves everything to a file. This is the only format that restores exactly.
- **Restore full backup** loads a backup file. It shows the counts first and only replaces after you confirm.
- CSV export is a spreadsheet view, not a full backup.

### Server & Compass
Sync status, conflicts and connections. It shows the server revision, the last confirmed server update, and the time of the latest saved record.
- Make sure **Use shared personal space** is chosen, so your phone and other devices share one copy on the server.
- If the status strip mentions "this browser", your changes are only on this device.

### Server sheet
A spreadsheet-style snapshot of the last saved server copy, which you can view or download. It isn't a live editor.

## Focus and check-ins

- Start focus on your Next thing to run a timer. **I'm drifting** offers a two-minute restart, a break, or an honest change of plan.
- When a booked calendar task's time starts, its timer starts on its own while Steadier is open.
- **Spoken check-ins:** Steadier says the task and the time left every 15 minutes for jobs of an hour or less, and every 30 minutes for longer jobs. It keeps the screen on while a calendar task runs, because phones pause apps when the screen sleeps.
- A timer never marks a task done by itself.

## Reminders

Give a booked task a reminder, for example 15 minutes before. Reminders work while Steadier is open. Calendar feed alerts depend on your calendar app's settings.

## The voice assistant

Hold the **+** button, or long-press the Steadier app icon and choose **Talk**. Speak, then answer **yes**, **no**, or a correction such as "make it 5". Nothing is saved until you say yes.

Things you can say:
- **Add a task or booking:** "Uber shift Friday 4 till 8, remind me 15 minutes before."
- **Several at once:** "I need to ring mum, pick up dog food tomorrow, and book the dentist for Friday at 10."
- **Mark done:** "Done with the mechanic call."
- **Move:** "Push the coffee to tomorrow morning." Repeating tasks have to be moved in the app.
- **Lists:** "Add eggs and bread to the shopping list." It goes on that list's run (one is started if needed).
- **Idea:** "Idea: trailer hire side business", or "add build a shed as an idea". It goes on Front of Mind, not into your tasks. Starting with "Idea" always makes it an idea.
- **Notes:** "Note: the car's making a rattle."
- **Check your day:** "What's next?" or "What's on today?"
- **Ask how to use Steadier:** "How do I move a task?" or "What's the Compass map for?"

"Morning" means 9 am, "afternoon" 2 pm, and "evening" 6 pm, unless you say a time.

## Claude connector

Claude can see your Front of Mind and which tasks are set up, and can suggest a Place or First step, but only you can make a task ready. Tasks Claude creates start as faded cards.

In any chat you can say "add that as an idea" and Claude adds it to Front of Mind, labelled **added by Claude** until you Keep it, with a backup saved first. Claude reads Front of Mind before advising. It can change an item's wording (the earlier wording stays in History) and suggest Let go (dashed), but only you can Keep, Let go, make a task or promote.

Claude can read your Link check. It can suggest a link between a task or project and an Imperative, but the link waits for your **Confirm**. Only you can choose Not yet, make a project Support, or step an Imperative down.

Claude can read and change your Steadier when you ask it to in a chat. For example: "What's on my calendar this week?", "Find duplicate tasks", "Add milk to the shopping list" (it shows dashed on the list until you confirm it), or "What did I note about the car?" Claude can also read your assets (names, condition, next due, last done, and the type and date of recent entries, never costs, prices, serial numbers, documents or notes) so a voice review can mention upkeep; it can't change them. Claude always asks before changing anything, and Steadier saves a full backup before every change. Connect it in Claude under **Settings → Connectors**.

## How do I…

- **Add a task?** Tap **+** on My life today, or tell the voice assistant. After the title, choose **Task** or **Idea** (Task is the default).
- **Set up a task / make it ready?** Open it, or use **Sort**, and light the three dots on its card: Action, Place, First step.
- **Link a project to an Imperative?** Open **Link check**, or open the project and pick under **Serves Imperative**.
- **Save an idea for later?** Tap **+** and choose **Idea**, or start with "Idea:" and it switches for you. You can also say "Idea: …" to the voice assistant, use Paste anything, or tell Claude "add that as an idea".
- **Find what Area a task had?** Look in its notes for a line like `Area: Home`.
- **Give a task a time on the calendar?** Open the task and set its day and start time, or say "book X on Friday at 3".
- **Change a booked time?** Open it from the Calendar and change the day or time, or say "move X to tomorrow at 9".
- **Delete a task?** Steadier doesn't delete a whole task with one tap, so nothing vanishes by accident. If it's finished, mark it **Done**. If you've decided not to do it, choose **Dropped**: this shows as "Still on, or dropped?" when you pick a task you've passed over a few times, and the task leaves your suggestions but stays in **All work** so you can bring it back.
- **Remove a task for good?** Ask Claude, for example "delete the task called old car rego". A **Delete / Keep it** card then appears on **My life today**. Tap **Delete** to confirm. A backup is saved first. The voice button can't delete.
- **Delete a subtask?** Open the main task, find it under **Subtasks**, and tap **Delete**.
- **Mark something done?** Tap **Done** on your Next thing, use the task's state, or say "done with X".
- **Make a task repeat?** Open the task and set its repeat (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or a number of days after you finish).
- **Find an old task?** Use **All work** and search.
- **Get my data onto a new phone?** Sign in, then choose **Server & Compass → Use shared personal space**.
- **Make a backup?** **Backups & trial → Download full backup**. The server also keeps daily backups.
- **See why something was suggested?** My life today shows a reason under each suggestion.
- **Undo a mistake?** Use undo in the page menu right away. For bigger mistakes, a server backup from before each change can be restored.
