# My Life: a one-week Steadier trial

Start with a week. Extend to a month only if it helps you notice relevant responsibilities, return to your chosen intention and recognise real progress. This is a trial of the system, not a test of your willpower.

## Start small

Open Preview and use the clearly labelled sample workspace to explore. Under **Backups & trial**, load a sample scenario if you want the new project framing. Then switch to **My personal space**. The sample controls cannot replace personal information.

Add five or so real responsibilities: something time-bound you must not miss, an ongoing support need, and a few steps towards an outcome you care about. Make one project with a short purpose, a visible desired outcome and one concrete milestone. You do not need to enter your whole life.

**Direction** describes what shapes, advances or protects your chosen life. **Support** describes what keeps it functioning. **Timely** means attention within a meaningful window. **Sustained** means continuing engagement. These are roles and attention patterns, not high/low value or urgent/not urgent. An ongoing support responsibility may contain a timely individual action.

Capture first, clarify later. Suggested identity is provisional. A real deadline comes from what you explicitly enter; the app does not look up expiry dates, licence rules or bills. Suggested preparation is a planning aid, not a second deadline. Check and change it when needed. Unclear work remains available in Review and the matrix's unclassified view.

## Each day: orient, choose, return

Open **My life today**. Check the independently visible commitments and any waiting conditions. Use Compass alongside the app to choose what deserves engagement. Save the choice in **What have I chosen with Compass?**, with an optional next task.

Begin with two minutes if that helps. **I'm drifting** pauses the timer and offers a smaller entry step, a break or a conscious plan change. The app remembers where you left the focus, but it does not know why you drifted, track other apps or choose on behalf of Compass.

When something changes, record evidence: “The bench is accessible” tells you more than a count of jobs completed. Projects hold milestones and observed progress. Completing a task does not automatically mean its larger outcome is achieved.

## Back up outside the browser

Your tasks and framework are saved automatically **on this browser and website address**. That is not a cloud backup. Clearing browser data can remove the workspace and its local recovery copies together. A different phone, browser, Preview address or published hostname has a separate store.

Open **Backups & trial → Download full backup** after meaningful changes, ideally at the end of each trial day. Check that the dated JSON file actually appears in Downloads, and keep copies in a private folder outside the browser. “Last download requested” does not prove the device saved the file. If download is blocked in the embedded app, **Copy backup text** is an alternative; save that text as a private `.json` file. Never paste it into a public site.

A full backup includes tasks, IDs and dependencies, life identity, purposes, projects, outcomes, milestones, progress, matrix emphasis, today's intention, focus and trial metadata. CSV is only a core-field interchange and is not a full backup. Additive import creates fresh task IDs and may add duplicates; full restore preserves the saved workspace exactly.

## Restore carefully

Download your current full backup first if possible. Choose **Restore full backup**, select a JSON file, inspect the task/project counts and target workspace, then explicitly confirm replacement. Selecting a file or cancelling does not replace anything. Full restore replaces the current selected workspace, not both personal and demo stores.

Up to three dated local recovery copies are available for preview and deliberate restore. They are convenient recovery points, not copies on another device. Before a deliberate restore the app attempts to preserve the current readable workspace. Malformed original data is protected; use **Download raw saved data** or restore a checked backup rather than clearing it casually.

Existing v1 data is copied forward to v2 with IDs intact; original v1 browser keys are not deleted or rewritten. New identity fields are neutral until clarified, rather than retrospectively asserting what your old tasks mean.

## At the end of the week

Ask: What did the system stop me overlooking? Did today's frame help me return? Could I see a meaningful change towards an outcome? Did entering and clarifying information become too much work? Was the backup process easy enough to repeat?

Note the review under **Backups & trial**, keep a full backup, then decide whether to extend to a month. Refine the friction you actually encountered rather than adding more features or importing a bigger backlog immediately.

## Limits to keep in mind

This is a development Preview, not a guaranteed always-on published service. It has no automatic cloud sync, scheduled off-device backups or dependable closed-app reminders. Keep existing calendar/reminder safeguards for critical obligations during the trial. A separately published stable website can be arranged later; this update does not publish your app or upload personal records.

Voice is optional, begins only when requested and depends on the browser. Speech recognition may require internet and may be processed by the browser vendor. Full-browser Android testing and your real-use experience remain necessary; software checks are not a claim of clinical ADHD benefit or measured attention improvement.
