# Steadier screen guide

Where to tap, screen by screen. Each screen says how to get there, what each button does and where it leads, and gives step-by-step walks. Words in **bold** are the exact labels on screen. "Leads to" names another screen in this guide, or "stays here".

For Claude: give one step at a time, then wait. Each step says what the owner should see next; check that before giving the next step. Use read_steadier_guide with screen set to a screen's name for just that screen.

## Screen: Getting around

Job: the parts that are on every page.
- **☰** (top left) opens the menu: every page in Steadier, in a list. At the bottom of the menu, **App options** opens the App menu. Leads to: the page you tap.
- Page name and date (top middle): shows where you are. Not a button.
- **⋮** (top right) opens This page: **Read aloud** (reads the page out), **Undo** (undoes your last change), **Theme** (light, dark or automatic) and, on some pages, extras such as **Export costs (CSV)** on Assets & Upkeep. Tap **Close** to shut it. Stays here.
- ⋮ › **Notifications** opens the phone notifications panel (Sheet: Notifications).
- **App options** (bottom of the ☰ menu) opens App: **Flag** (the unplaced list), **Connection**, and on Today **Say intention**, **Re-anchor** and **Backups**. Elsewhere it has **Speak** instead.
- The **i** (right side, under the header) opens "What this page is for", the page's explanation. Tap it again to fold it. Stays here.
- Bottom bar, left to right: **Today**, **Overview**, **All work**, **Calendar**, **Projects**. Leads to: that screen.
- **+** (bottom right, in the bar): tap to capture by typing, hold to talk to the voice assistant. Leads to: Sheet: Capture.
- Older pages (those with "My life /" and the page name at the top) have no ⋮: their Theme switch is in the top bar.
- Back: the device back button, or the page's own back link (for example **All lists** or **All assets**).

## Screen: Today

Job: doing. Top to bottom: Now, Next thing, Any time today, the week, then the day in time order.
Get there: bottom bar › **Today** (1st). Or ☰ › **My life today**.
- Check-in line (at the top, weekdays from 08:00 and 17:00, only when there's a question): tap it to answer, or tap **Later**. Stays here.
- **Needs attention (n)**: under the check-in line. Opens Overview with its list. Leads to: Screen: Overview.
- Now (only while something is running): the booked block in progress, and your focus timer if it's on a different task (both show, each labelled). Buttons: **Done** (ticks it off, stays here), **Finished early** on a booking, **Return to focus** on the focus card. Nothing running = no Now.
- Next thing · your choice: the one you chose, with **Start** (starts the focus timer, stays here) and **Change**. None chosen: **Choose…**, tap a task, check its first small action, then **Make it my Next thing**. Stays here.
- Any time today: tasks dated today with no clock time. Tap the circle to tick one off, or the row to open it. Leads to: Sheet: Task.
- Week strip: Mon–Sun with today highlighted and a grey mark for booked time. Display only.
- Agenda: today's bookings in time order, with the red now line. Tap a row to open it (leads to Sheet: Task), or its circle to tick it off. Past and done rows are faded.
- A row such as "Comwireit Cleaning in 1h 22m" sits above the next booking, with "leave by 10:35" in grey at a saved place that has an Allow to get there time.
- **Free · 2h 15m** rows mark gaps of over an hour. **+ Add** opens capture with "today at" and that time filled in. Leads to: Sheet: Capture.
- A list chip such as "Packing 3/7" opens that list. Leads to: Screen: Lists.
- Slipped row (past its likely finish, in the agenda): **Keep going** (stretches today's block to its likely finish, stays here), **Move** (opens the task to change its time, leads to Sheet: Task editor), **Drop** (off today, kept in All work).
- Blocked row: **Move it** or **Remove prerequisite**.

### Walk: Move today's block
- Step 1. On Today, tap the block's row. You should see its task sheet slide up.
- Step 2. Tap **Edit** at the bottom of the sheet. You should see the task editor.
- Step 3. Find **When**. Either type the new time in plain words, for example "today 3pm", or change **Start** (and **Day** for another day).
- Step 4. Wait for **Saved** at the bottom, then tap **Close**. You should be back on Today with the block at its new time.
- If the block has already slipped, its row shows **Move**: tap it to go straight to step 3, or tap **Keep going** to stretch it instead.

## Screen: Overview

Job: seeing.
Get there: bottom bar › **Overview** (2nd). Or tap **Needs attention (n)** on Today.
- Needs attention rows (only rows above zero): **Review due** (leads to Screen: Review due), **Link check** (Screen: Link check), **Weekly review** (Screen: Weekly Review), **Sort (not set up)** (Screen: Sort), **Unplaced** (opens the unplaced list), **Upkeep due** (opens in place; each item leads to Screen: Assets & Upkeep), **Past due** and Claude requests.
- Next thing: your chosen one. The Pool under it has **Not now**, **Still on** and **Drop** on each task.
- **Today**: booked count and free time, with a strip of the day. Leads to: Screen: Calendar.
- **Moving**: up to three active projects, then All projects. Leads to: Screen: Projects.
- **Held and waiting**, then **Why**: Compass, Pillars, Front of Mind and Movement. Front of Mind leads to Screen: Front of Mind; Compass leads to Screen: Compass map.

## Screen: All work

Job: finding any task.
Get there: bottom bar › **All work** (3rd). Or ☰ › **All work**.
- Search box (top): type to find a task. Stays here.
- Filter (next to search): one filter, for example **Someday** or **Waiting**. Stays here.
- Group headings (by project, then **No project**): tap to fold or open.
- Round tick (left of a row): marks it done. Stays here.
- Row: opens it. Leads to: Sheet: Task.

## Screen: Calendar

Job: when.
Get there: bottom bar › **Calendar** (4th). Or ☰ › **Calendar**. Or tap the day strip on Overview.
- **Day**, **Week**, **Board** (top): switch the view. Stays here.
- Arrows either side of the date: previous or next day or week. **Today** jumps back to today.
- A block: opens its task. Leads to: Sheet: Task.
- Board: a list of today's bookings with a status, such as "~10:55 · +10m" when one is running 10 minutes or more late.

## Screen: Projects

Job: the projects themselves.
Get there: bottom bar › **Projects** (5th). Or ☰ › **Project Outcomes**.
- A project row: opens the project page. Leads to: Screen: Project page.

## Screen: Project page

Get there: Projects › tap a project.
- Shows **Next step**, **Why**, **Outcome** and its tasks. **+ Add** fills an empty field.
- A task: leads to Sheet: Task.
- A list attached to the project: **Start a run** leads to Screen: Lists.

## Sheet: Task

Opens over the page when you tap a task.
- Shows the task's photos, **Next action**, **Notes**, **Subtasks** and any lists.
- **Read aloud** (bottom): reads the task out. Stays here.
- **Edit** (bottom): leads to Sheet: Task editor.
- Close: swipe down or tap outside the sheet.

## Sheet: Task editor

Job: change one task. It saves by itself: the line under the title says **Saving…**, then **Saved**.
- Top: the done circle (left), the title, and the **i** (right), which opens voice: **Speak task update**, the dictation notes and **Read next step**.
- Voice draft: anything you dictate waits in a dashed box, **Voice draft, not saved yet**. **Keep it** saves it; **Undo** puts the task back.
- **When**: the summary (e.g. "Wed 25 Nov · 9am–4pm · 7h"), a box to type when in plain words, then **Day** (or **This one's day** on a repeat), **Start** and **Length** in hours and minutes. **Booked on the calendar** makes it a booking on Today and the Calendar. **Fixed date/time** or **Flexible time window** switches the kind of timing.
- **Repeat**: shows its summary (e.g. "Every Wed"); tap to open the settings, including **Skip this week's instance** and **Ends on** / **Maximum occurrences (optional)**.
- **Reminders**: add one, for example 15 min before. They ring in Steadier while it's open, and as a phone notification when it's closed.
- **Place**, then **Next physical step**: tick **Title is the step** when the title says it all; untick to write a different step.
- **Where it belongs**: shows where it sits (an Imperative, project or catch-all); tap to open Imperatives, project, catch-all, **Role**, **Attention pattern** and why it belongs. A green bar means it serves an Imperative.
- **Notes**, **Subtasks**, **Small steps** and attached lists.
- **More** (folded): **Finish by** (date and time), **Not before** (hidden from your lists until then), **Start preparing from**, **Next review**, minutes for the next step (only when it isn't booked with a length), Context, Workflow, **Waiting on**, prerequisites, **Decision outcome**, **Pin to attention**, **Major anchor (show in Month)**, **Evidence of progress** and **Activity**.
- **Close** (bottom): back to where you were.

## Sheet: Capture

Opens with **+** (tap).
- **Task** or **Idea** (top): choose what you're adding.
- Type in the box. With Task, it saves by itself after a pause, or tap **Save & close**. With Idea, tap **Save to Front of Mind**.
- Pasting several things offers to split them into items, each with a tick box.
- Hold **+** instead of tapping to talk.

### Walk: Add an idea
- Step 1. Tap **+** at the bottom right. You should see the capture box.
- Step 2. Tap **Idea** at the top. It should look picked.
- Step 3. Type the idea.
- Step 4. Tap **Save to Front of Mind**. Closing the box also saves it. You'll find it on Screen: Front of Mind.
- By voice: hold **+** and say "Idea: …".

## Sheet: Notifications

Phone notifications that arrive when Steadier is closed. Only two kinds: a task's own reminders, and leave by.
Get there: ⋮ (top right, any page) › **Notifications**.
- First line: whether this phone is on. **Turn on** asks the phone for permission and signs it up; **Turn off** stops this phone.
- **Task reminders**: on or off. **Leave by**: on or off.
- **Quiet hours**: on or off, with From and To times. A reminder in quiet hours is dropped, not held.
- **Send a test**: sends "Steadier test" to every phone that's on. It should arrive in a few seconds.
- **Close** shuts the panel.

### Walk: Turn on phone notifications
- Step 1. Open Steadier on the phone. Tap ⋮ at the top right, then **Notifications**. You should see "Off on this phone."
- Step 2. Tap **Turn on**. The phone asks to allow notifications: tap Allow. The line should change to "On for this phone."
- Step 3. Tap **Send a test**. Within a few seconds a "Steadier test" notification should appear. Close Steadier and it still works.
- If it says "Blocked for Steadier in the phone's settings", open the phone's Settings › Apps › Steadier › Notifications, allow them, and start again.

## Screen: Sort

Job: one minute a day to set up faded tasks.
Get there: Overview › **Sort (not set up)**, or ☰ › **Sort**.
- One faded card at a time. **Place**: pick a project, Life Admin or Erosion Guard (**Other project…** for more). **First step**: say or type it, or tap **Title is the step**. **It's an action** confirms the title reads as something to do.
- **Skip**: next card, nothing changed. **Done**: next card.
- After the last card you go back to Today.

## Screen: Front of Mind

Job: what's on your mind that isn't a task yet.
Get there: ☰ › **Front of Mind**, or Overview › Why › Front of Mind.
- Tap a row to open its actions: **Make it a task** (opens a task draft), **Promote to Imperative** (saves a draft for your Weekly Review), **Keep**, **Let go**. Also **Edit wording** and History.
- Let go (n) at the bottom: the items you let go, each with **Bring back**.
- Add one with **+** › **Idea** (see Walk: Add an idea).

## Screen: Review due

Job: quick check-ins on work due for review, and unplaced work.
Get there: Overview › **Review due**, or ☰ › **Review due / unplaced**.
- The review card at the top starts the Weekly Review when it's due. Leads to: Screen: Weekly Review.
- Tap a row to open it. Leads to: Sheet: Task. The tick box completes it.

## Screen: Weekly Review

Job: turn the week's reality into what you'll do. Ten steps: Start, Unplaced, Front of Mind, Link check, Money, Action, Capacity, Imperatives, Map, Save.
Get there: Overview › **Weekly review** (when due), the review card on Review due, or ☰ › **Weekly Reality Review**.
- **Step n of 10** (top): opens the list of steps. Tap a step to jump to it.
- **Begin** (on Start) then **Next** (bottom right): the next step. **Back** (bottom left): the step before.
- **Answer by voice** and **Dictate**: speak an answer or note.
- Action step: for each commitment, **Yes, done**, **Not yet** or **Drop to Someday**.
- Imperatives step: drafts from Front of Mind offer **Add as Imperative** or **Not now**.
- Map step: **Open the Compass map (your answers are kept)**.
- Save step: notes, a summary, then **Save review**. Leads to: Screen: Today.
- Your answers are kept on this device until you save, so you can leave and come back.

### Walk: Do the Weekly Review
- Step 1. Tap **Overview** on the bottom bar, then **Weekly review**. You should see "Step 1 of 10" and **Begin**.
- Step 2. Read the Start step, then tap **Begin**. You should see Step 2 of 10, Unplaced.
- Step 3. Answer each step, then tap **Next**. Tap **Back** to change an earlier answer, or tap **Step n of 10** to jump straight to a step.
- Step 4. On the last step, Save, add any notes, check the summary, then tap **Save review**. You should land on Today. If it says the review was not saved, your answers are kept: check the connection and tap **Save review** again.

## Screen: Link check

Job: Imperatives with no work, Imperative projects with no Imperative, and projects with no Goal Outcome.
Get there: Overview › **Link check**, or ☰ › **Link check**. It's also the Link check step of the Weekly Review.
- Standard header: the page name and date at the top, the **i** for its explanation, and ⋮ with **Read aloud**, **Undo** and **Theme**.
- An Imperative with no work: **Link work** (pick **A project** or **Or a task**), **Not yet** (held until your next Weekly Review), **Step down** (**Sustain**, **Delegate or minimise** or **Retire**). **Cancel** closes a picker.
- An Imperative project with no Imperative: a **Suggested** line with **Confirm**, **Link to an Imperative** (then tap the Imperative), **Make it Support**, **Not yet**.
- A project with no Goal Outcome: **Open project**. Leads to: Screen: Project page.
- In the Weekly Review, Not yet reads **Not yet again**.

### Walk: Confirm a suggested link
- Step 1. Open Link check. Find the project with a grey **Suggested** line naming an Imperative.
- Step 2. Tap **Confirm** on that line. The project leaves the list, and a line says it's confirmed.

### Walk: Link work to an Imperative
- Step 1. On Link check, under Imperatives with no work, tap **Link work** on the Imperative.
- Step 2. Choose from **A project** or **Or a task**. It links as soon as you pick, and the Imperative leaves the list.

### Walk: Not yet
- Step 1. On Link check, tap **Not yet** on the Imperative or project. It's held, and comes back in your next Weekly Review's Link check step.

### Walk: Make a project Support
- Step 1. On Link check, under Imperative projects with no Imperative, tap **Make it Support** on the project. It becomes a Support project and leaves the list.

## Screen: Imperatives

Job: what you can't responsibly ignore right now.
Get there: ☰ › **Imperatives**.
- Each card: its reason, "Set 27 Oct", projects and open steps (tap a step for Sheet: Task), and On the map.
- On the map: tap a goal to open it (Screen: Compass item). **Unlink** removes a direct link. A row saying "via Move house" is reached through that project and has no Unlink.
- **+ Link**: opens the picker. Choose, then **Link**, or **Cancel**.
- **Edit**: opens the card's fields (below). It changes to **Close**.
- **Retire…**: asks first. **Retire** moves it to Retired, **Cancel** leaves it.
- **+ Add an Imperative** (bottom): **What I cannot ignore**, **Short reason**, **Notion page link (optional)**, then **Add**.
- Retired (n) at the bottom: tap to show them, each with **Make active again**.

### Walk: Edit an Imperative's title and reason
- Step 1. Open ☰ › **Imperatives**. Find the card and tap **Edit**. You should see Title and Why I cannot ignore this.
- Step 2. Change **Title**. It saves when you leave the box (tap the next box).
- Step 3. Change **Why I cannot ignore this**. It saves the same way.
- Step 4. Tap **Close** on the card.

### Walk: Link an Imperative to a Pillar
- Step 1. On the Imperative's card, tap **+ Link**. You should see "Link to a Pillar, Value Goal or Outcome".
- Step 2. Choose the Pillar from the list.
- Step 3. Tap **Link**. The Pillar appears under On the map with **Unlink**.

### Walk: Set an Imperative's decision
- Step 1. On the card, tap **Edit**.
- Step 2. In **Decision outcome**, choose **Promote**, **Sustain**, **Delegate or minimise** or **Retire**. **Not decided** clears it. Choosing Retire here records the decision; it doesn't retire the card.
- Step 3. If Claude suggested one, it shows as suggested with **Confirm**. Tap it to make it yours.
- Step 4. Tap **Close**. The card shows "Decision: …".

## Screen: Compass map

Job: Pillars, Value Goals and Goal Outcomes, and how projects serve them.
Get there: ☰ › **Compass map**, Overview › Why › Compass, or **Open Compass map** on Pillars & Goals.
- Top: **Read aloud** and **Compass view**. The Pillar strip shows your Pillars. A free slot adds one: it scrolls to Add to the map.
- The tree: tap an item to open it. Leads to: Screen: Compass item.
- Gaps: things missing a link, with **Read gaps** and a show-all switch.
- Add to the map (bottom): choose **Pillar**, **Value Goal** or **Goal Outcome**, then the title, **What it means (optional)**, the items it sits under, then **Add Pillar** (or **Add Value Goal**, **Add Goal Outcome**).

### Walk: Add a Pillar with its definition
- Step 1. Open ☰ › **Compass map** and scroll to Add to the map at the bottom.
- Step 2. Tap **Pillar**. Type its name in **Pillar title**.
- Step 3. Type the definition in **What it means (optional)**.
- Step 4. Tap **Add Pillar**. It appears in the Pillar strip and the tree.
- For a Pillar you already have: tap it on the map, change **What it means (optional)** under Details, then tap **Save changes**.

### Walk: Add a Value Goal
- Step 1. On the Compass map, at Add to the map, tap **Value Goal**.
- Step 2. Type it in **Value Goal title**, and the meaning if you like.
- Step 3. Under Sits under, tap the Pillar(s) it belongs to. Each one you tap looks picked.
- Step 4. Tap **Add Value Goal**.

### Walk: Add a Goal Outcome
- Step 1. On the Compass map, at Add to the map, tap **Goal Outcome**.
- Step 2. Type it in **Goal Outcome title**, for example "New lease signed by March".
- Step 3. Under Sits under, tap the Value Goal(s) it serves.
- Step 4. Tap **Add Goal Outcome**. To have a project serve it, open it (Screen: Compass item) and use **Add existing project…**.
- Shortcut: open a Value Goal, and under Goal Outcomes under this type in the new title, then tap **Add new**.

## Screen: Compass item

One Pillar, Value Goal or Goal Outcome.
Get there: tap an item on the Compass map, on Pillars & Goals, or under On the map on Imperatives.
- Back link (top left): back where you came from. **Save changes** (top) saves edits. **Read aloud**.
- Sits under: each parent with **Unlink**, or **Confirm** / **Reject** when suggested. Also sits under… and **Link** add another.
- Details: **Title**, **What it means (optional)**, **Notion page link (optional)**, **Save changes**, and Retire (kept, not deleted).
- Under this: the items below it, and a new one with **Add new**.
- On an Outcome, Projects serving this: **Add existing project…**.
- Imperatives: the Imperatives that reach this item.

## Screen: Pillars & Goals

Job: your Pillars and what sits under each.
Get there: ☰ › **Pillars & Goals**. Or **Pillars & Goals** (back link) from a Compass item opened here.
- One card per Pillar, in your own order. Each shows its name, its definition (or **+ Add definition**) and what's under it, e.g. "1 Value Goal · 1 Outcome · 2 projects". A green bar: an active Imperative is linked to it or something under it. Grey chips only when something's off: "no definition", "no Value Goal", "quiet 30d".
- Tap a card to open it: its Value Goals, under each its Goal Outcomes, under each the projects serving it. Tap any name to open it (Screen: Compass item, or the project).
- **+ Add Value Goal** and **+ Add Outcome**: type one and press Enter for the next, or paste several lines at once; tap **Add** or **Done**. Tap the definition to change it, then **Save**.
- **Retired (n)** (bottom) lists retired Pillars. **Open Compass map ›** leads to Screen: Compass map.

### Walk: Fill in a Pillar
- Step 1. Open ☰ › **Pillars & Goals** and tap the Pillar's card. It opens.
- Step 2. Tap **+ Add definition**, type it, and press Enter (or **Save**).
- Step 3. Tap **+ Add Value Goal**, type it, and press Enter. The box stays open for another; tap **Done** when finished.
- Step 4. Under that Value Goal, tap **+ Add Outcome**, type it, and press Enter.
- Several at once: paste them into the box, one per line, then press Enter.

## Screen: Lists

Job: reusable checklists. Each use is a run with its own ticks and date.
Get there: ☰ › **Lists**, a list chip on a task card, or a list on a task, project or note.
- **+ List**: name it, choose its type, then **Make list** or **Cancel**.
- Lists are grouped In use and Ready. Tap one to open it.
- In a list: **All lists** (top left) goes back. Three tabs: **Run**, **Template**, Runs (n).
- Template: the items and sections. **Start a run** (or Run in progress › if one is open). Add items with the New item box.
- Run: a progress bar; for Do-Confirm lists a Next card with **Done**; each item's tick box; **Reset** (untick all, asks first); **New run**; Add for this run only, then **Add**; **Finish**.
- After Finish: Anything missing? offers items you added this run, with **Add to list**, then **Done** (or **Nothing missing**).

### Walk: Start a list run, tick, finish
- Step 1. Open ☰ › **Lists** and tap the list. You should see its Template.
- Step 2. Tap **Start a run**. You should see the Run tab with a progress bar.
- Step 3. Tap each item's tick box as you do it, or tap **Done** on the Next card. The bar fills.
- Step 4. Tap **Finish** at the bottom. You should see Finished, with the date and count.
- Step 5. If Anything missing? lists something you added just for this run, tap **Add to list** to keep it for next time. Then tap **Done**, or **Nothing missing**.

## Screen: Assets & Upkeep

Job: things you look after: vehicles, places, equipment, appliances.
Get there: ☰ › **Assets & Upkeep**, or Overview › **Upkeep due** › an item.
- The list is grouped by type. Tap one to open its page. Retired (n) is at the bottom.
- On an asset: **All assets** (top left) goes back. **Odometer** or **Engine hours** with **+ Reading**. Service plans with **Log it done**. Revisit with **Record check**. History with **+ Log entry**. Retire at the bottom.
- Forms end with **Save** and **Cancel**.
- ⋮ › **Export costs (CSV)** when costs are logged.

### Walk: Enter an odometer reading
- Step 1. Open ☰ › **Assets & Upkeep** and tap the vehicle.
- Step 2. Under **Odometer**, tap **+ Reading**. You should see Reading (km) and Date.
- Step 3. Type the reading, check the date, then tap **Save**. "Update reading" leaves Upkeep due.

### Walk: Log a service
- Step 1. On the vehicle's page, find the service plan and tap **Log it done**. Or, under History, tap **+ Log entry** and choose **Service**.
- Step 2. Check **Service plan**, set **Date**, and fill in **Reading (km, optional)** to record the odometer at the same time.
- Step 3. Add **Cost (optional)**, **Who did it (optional)** and **Notes (optional)** if you like.
- Step 4. Tap **Save**. The plan's next due date moves on, and the entry shows in History.

## Screen: Near me

Job: places and the tasks at them.
Get there: ☰ › **Near me**.
- Tasks near you, grouped by place (needs location permission).
- Your places (n): tap to open the list. Each place has **Arrive within** (the arrival distance), **Allow to get there** (time to allow, for "leave by" on Today) and a bin button to remove it.
- **Add a place**: search an address or business, name it, then save.

### Walk: Set a place's Allow to get there time
- Step 1. Open ☰ › **Near me**, then tap Your places (n) to open it.
- Step 2. Find the place. In **Allow to get there**, choose a time, for example 20 min. **Not set** turns it off.
- Step 3. That's it: it saves at once. Booked tasks at that place now show "leave by …" on Today until they start.

## Screen: Obligations

Job: bills, licences, renewals, insurance, tax and cancel-by dates.
Get there: ☰ › **Obligations**.
- Search obligations, the list, and add or edit one (**Name**, **Type**, **Cadence**, **Next action**, **Prepare from**, **Linked to**). Scan a bill fills one in from a photo; amounts are kept from Claude if you've chosen that.

## Screen: Notes / Daily Log

Job: notes to self by day.
Get there: ☰ › **Notes / Daily Log**.
- **Your words** and **Entry date**, then save. **Clear draft** empties the box. Recent entries below, each with edit and **Delete entry**.

## Screen: Backups & trial

Get there: ☰ › **Backups & trial**, or ☰ › **App options** › **Backups** on Today.
- Make a full backup, restore one, and trial settings. CSV alone is not a full backup.

## Screen: Server & Compass

Get there: ☰ › **Server & Compass**, or App options › **Connection**.
- Sync with the server, conflicts, the latest record time, and connector access.

## Screen: Older pages

Short entries for pages you rarely use. Each opens from ☰.
- **Waiting & blocked**: tasks waiting on someone or something. Tap a task to open it.
- **Completed steps**: what you've finished, newest first. Look-back only.
- **Compass view**: active Imperatives and projects with their signals. **Compass map** is a richer view.
- **My life matrix**: balance across Imperative/Support and Timely/Sustained, as counts and time. No ranking.
- **Time / plans**: the week's time against planned work.
- **Server sheet**: a read-only snapshot of the last confirmed server copy.
- **Decision Log**: decisions saved in Weekly Reviews. Read-only.
- **Work Pipeline**: projects and their tasks together, by stage.
- **Routine Runner**: run a repeating task's checklist.
- **Year Wheel**: seasonal reviews, tasks, projects and obligations across the year.
- **Move & Energy**: movement and energy windows, kept only in this browser.
- **Weather / Forecast**: a forecast for a chosen place, kept only in this browser.
- **People & Follow-ups**: people and what you promised them; it will serve your Friends & Relations outcome as a ledger of who, and when you last made contact.
- **Credential renewal dates**: renewal names and dates, never the secret values.
- **Invoices**: create and export invoice records.

Retired 17 Nov (off the menu; their data is kept and they can be brought back): Evidence Feed and Site Cards.

## Screen: Outside the app

- Sign-in: your email and password.
- Voice page (/voice): talk to Steadier. Hold the mic, then speak. It can also answer "How do I…" questions.
- Download (/download): the Android app.
- Guide (/guide): the user guide; Screens (/guide/screens): this guide.
